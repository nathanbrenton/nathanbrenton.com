Privacy policy

This site has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The only personal details I get are the ones you choose to send me.

Last updated November 23, 2026

Who I am

nathanbrenton.com belongs to Nathan Brenton, an independent IT and digital media consultant in Orange County, California. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Nathan Brenton. Questions about your information go to info@nathanbrenton.com.

What this site doesn't do

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It runs no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • It loads nothing from other companies: no outside fonts, scripts or embedded videos. Everything comes from nathanbrenton.com.
  • I don't sell or share personal information, and I don't add anyone to a mailing list.

What you send me

When you use the quote form or email me, I receive what you write: your name, email address and, if you give them, your phone number, organization, city or ZIP, and the details of the job.

The quote form is sent from my web server through Postmark, an email delivery service, to my business inbox. Email to info@nathanbrenton.com is received by my server and forwarded to the same inbox. My server doesn't store what you type into the form. Postmark keeps a copy of each message it delivers for a limited time as a delivery record.

I use these details only to reply, prepare a quote and do the work you ask for.

Server logs

Like almost every web server, mine records each request it receives: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and the browser and referring page your browser reports. I use these logs to keep the site secure and working, for example to block spam sent through the form and to fix errors. They are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Who else handles it

A few service providers handle information only so they can provide their service to me:

  • the company I rent the web server from
  • Postmark, which delivers the quote form's messages
  • my email provider, which holds my business inbox

I would also disclose information if the law required it.

How long I keep it

I keep quote requests and emails while we're talking. If you become a client, I keep what's needed for the work and for my business and tax records. Otherwise I'll delete your messages when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email info@nathanbrenton.com and I'll reply within 30 days. I may need to keep some records the law requires, and I'll tell you if that applies.

Please don't send passwords, account numbers or other sensitive details through the form or by email. If a job needs them, we'll arrange a safer way.

Other sites, children and changes

Links to YouTube, GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
