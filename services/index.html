IT

IT support and migrations

Help for the computers, accounts and network a small office depends on, on-site in Orange County or remotely.

  • Computer, printer and Wi-Fi set-up and troubleshooting
  • Email and file moves between providers, without losing history
  • Domain, DNS and email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Backups you can actually restore, tested
  • Account and access audits: who controls what, and moving ownership back to you
  • Taking over from a former IT person or web vendor, with a written hand-over

Web

Websites

Hand-built sites that load fast, have nothing to patch every week, and stay in your name.

  • New sites, and rebuilds of sites stuck on an aging platform or host
  • Moves that keep your old links and Google search history working
  • Contact and request forms that reach your inbox, with spam blocked and no picture puzzles
  • HTTPS, security headers and uptime monitoring
  • Search basics: page titles, structured data, sitemap and Search Console
  • Privacy policy and accessibility checks
  • Ongoing care: small edits, monitoring and a monthly check

Media

Photo and video

Real pictures of your team, your work and your space, for the website, listings and social posts.

  • Team, workplace and on-the-job photos, edited and delivered for web and print
  • Before-and-after and product photos
  • Short brand and how-to videos, with clean audio and captions
  • Horizontal and vertical cuts for the website and social feeds
  • Model and property releases handled

Presence

Social and listings

Make sure people who search for you find the right hours, photos and reviews.

  • Google Business Profile set-up, clean-up and ownership checks
  • Yelp and other listings kept consistent
  • Review widgets for your site and review QR codes
  • Social account set-up with proper roles, not shared passwords
  • Monthly posting and photo updates

Process

How a job runs

  1. Request. Send the details through the quote form.
  2. Call. A short call or visit to understand the job.
  3. Written quote. Scope, price and timeline in writing before work starts.
  4. The work. Updates as it goes, nothing changes on your accounts without your OK.
  5. Hand-over. A short write-up of what was done, where things are and who has access.
